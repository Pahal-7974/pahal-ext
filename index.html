About

I’m originally from India and have been based in the Netherlands for the last 4 years. I’m at the beginning of my career and documenting the journey online, part virtual diary, part portfolio. I graduated cum laude with Honours in Computer Science (with a minor in Economics) in 2025, and finished my Master in Economics in 2026.

Before you ask, it wasn’t a pivot. A summer school at UCL on sustainability and economics sparked my interest in pluralist economics, which led to the minor and then the Master. I’ve combined both fields ever since, from my (Bachelor’s) Thesis Internship at PharmAccess to my Master’s Thesis on inequality in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh.

I’m working towards a career at the intersection of technology and economics, creating real impact through technological solutions. Today that means building LLM-based solutions at Datacation.
